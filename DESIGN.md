## Brand & Style

This design system elevates the digital identity of an established beauty, wellness, and advanced clinical cosmetology centre in Mallorca with over 23 years of craft. The visual tone balances clinical efficacy with Mediterranean tranquility—replacing outdated corporate aesthetics with organic warmth, tactile sophistication, and serene spaciousness.

### Design Movement: Mediterranean Organic Minimalism with Frosted Glass Touches
- **Purity & Natural Warmth:** A foundation of sand, linen, and warm calcareous neutrals avoids sterile clinical whites while maintaining pristine hygiene.
- **Botanical Precision:** Deep sage teal and soft sea glass tones communicate botanical therapies, thermal waters, and high-tech skincare devices without aggressive neon cues.
- **Refinement:** High-contrast editorial typography paired with subtle champagne gold highlights conveys premium artisanal care and time-tested credibility.
- **Glass & Tactility:** Gentle translucent backdrops mimic frosted apothecary glass and luminous Mediterranean sunlight.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from Mallorcan limestone, sea-washed sea glass, and native sage herbs.

- **Primary (`#0F766E` Deep Sea Teal):** Conveys scientific authority, botanical grounding, and calming serenity. Used for primary CTAs, active states, key titles, and essential brand indicators.
- **Secondary (`#C29B38` Warm Muted Gold):** Represents luxury, 23 years of premium pedigree, and subtle Mediterranean sunlight. Used strictly for accent badges, delicate borders, rating stars, and testimonial quotes.
- **Tertiary (`#14B8A6` Soft Emerald / Sea Glass):** Lighter clinical aqua used for interactive states, chip selections, and soft decorative background glows.
- **Neutral Backgrounds (`#FAF7F2` Calcareous Sand & `#F3ECE2` Warm Linen):** Replace cold `#FFFFFF` fields to evoke natural stone interiors, linen robes, and calm daylight.
- **Charcoal Text (`#1E293B` Slate Charcoal):** Provides deep, accessible contrast for microcopy and body text without the harshness of pure black.

## Typography

The type scale contrasts classical editorial luxury (**Playfair Display**) with crisp, geometric legibility (**Plus Jakarta Sans**).

- **Headlines & Display:** Playfair Display introduces bespoke editorial rhythm, capturing the quiet indulgence of high-end Mediterranean spas and medical-grade aesthetics. Its italic variations can be selectively utilized for poetic focal phrases (e.g., *«Tu oasis de belleza»*).
- **Body & UI Elements:** Plus Jakarta Sans provides contemporary digital ergonomics, rendering treatment specifications, prices, contraindications, and schedules cleanly across high-density mobile viewports.
- **Uppercase Labels:** Micro-badges, category tags, and status labels utilize `label-sm` or `label-md` with positive tracking (4–6%) for structured architectural restraint.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column responsive fluid grid with generous vertical whitespace mirrors the calm, uncluttered atmosphere of treatment suites.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with `margin: 3rem` (or max-width container of 1240px) and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Service matrices format in 3 or 4 balanced columns; specialized technology and home-service highlights use asymmetrical 7/5 or 6/6 splits.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`. Cards shift to 2-column distributions.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`. Critical booking action triggers dock to a fixed bottom glass bar to accommodate mobile booking behaviors.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Section headers require `space-xl` separation to maintain visual poise. Elements within cards maintain tight `space-sm` to `space-md` inner padding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth combines warm atmospheric illumination with frosted translucent overlays rather than heavy grey dropshadows.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Neutral canvas tinted in `#FAF7F2`. No elevation.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Organic Lift):** Used for service cards, apparatus showcases, and pricing tiles. Features a dual shadow tinted with warm umber and deep teal: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 118, 110, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.03);` bordered by a 1px solid `#F3ECE2` stroke.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Modal Elevation):** Interactive cards elevate smoothly on hover: `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -4px rgba(15, 118, 110, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.04); transform: translateY(-3px);`.
- **Glassmorphic Floating Bars (Nav & Mobile Sticky Booking):** Semi-opaque tinted surface (`rgba(250, 247, 242, 0.85)`) backed with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a whisper border of `rgba(194, 155, 56, 0.2)`.

## Shapes

Organic geometry uses soft, balanced radiuses that feel tactile and skin-friendly without appearing juvenile.

- **Cards & Visual Containers:** Standard corner radius is `16px` (`rounded-lg`), delivering an approachable, serene contour.
- **Pills & Badges:** Trust chips (e.g., "23 Años de Experiencia", "Servicio a Domicilio") and small interactive filters leverage full pill radii (`9999px`) to emphasize organic fluidity.
- **Media Portals:** Treatment photography alternates between rounded rectangles (`16px`) and arched portrait crops (`border-radius: 120px 120px 16px 16px`) for an elevated architectural Mediterranean sensibility.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Action ("Reservar Cita"):** Solid `#0F766E` background with white text (`label-md`), `padding: 0.875rem 1.75rem`, `border-radius: 9999px`. On hover, subtle luminance shift to `#14B8A6` with an expansion of ambient teal glow.
- **Secondary Action ("Saber más / Tratamientos"):** Transparent background with a refined `1.5px` border in `#0F766E`, text in `#0F766E`. Hover fills with a light sea wash (`rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.08)`).
- **Direct WhatsApp Floating Action:** Dedicated floating circle (`56px × 56px`) or anchored bottom-pill featuring `#25D366` background, crisp white icon, and micro-label with subtle pulse animation.

### Service & Clinical Cards
- Structure: Rounded `16px` surface finished in `#FFFFFF` with delicate outer border in `#F3ECE2`.
- Photography: Top-aligned image with an inset zoom effect on hover (`scale: 1.03`, transition: `400ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
- Typography & Hierarchy: Category micro-label in `label-sm` muted gold, followed by `headline-sm` Playfair Display title, brief benefit description in `body-md`, and an anchored text-link with an animated arrow.

### Trust Badges & Experience Seals
- **23-Year Heritage Badge:** Distinctive circular or pill crest featuring warm sand background, `#C29B38` fine border, gold iconography (olive branch or delicate numeral flourish), and dark slate typography.
- **At-Home Service ("A Domicilio") Badge:** Soft sage pill (`rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.15)`) with `#0F766E` text and a delicate home/hearth icon.

### Form Inputs & Appointment Selectors
- Background filled in `#FAF7F2`, bordered with `1px` `#E2D9CC`.
- Focus state activates an outline in `#0F766E` alongside a diffuse `0 0 0 3px rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.2)` ring.
- Floating labels transition smoothly from `body-md` down to `label-sm`.

### Testimonial & Quote Blocks
- Styled with warm sand backgrounds (`#F3ECE2`), oversized quotation marks styled in `#C29B38` at 30% opacity, and Playfair Display italic body text for humanized warmth and confidence.
